# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Zagaday is a small project with a public face. Everyone interacting with it — in issues, pull requests, discussions or support email — is expected to be respectful, direct and constructive.

Unacceptable: harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing others' private information.

## Scope

Applies to all Zagaday repositories, issue trackers, and any space where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to **a.kogut01@gmail.com**. Reports are handled confidentially and responded to within 72 hours.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block accounts that violate this policy.
